# Imagens fictícias do Pulso

Criadas com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens. As pessoas e os ambientes são fictícios. Os arquivos foram incorporados ao projeto e funcionam sem conexão com a internet.

## musculacao

Arquivo: `assets/imagens/musculacao.png`.

Prompt utilizado:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide editorial photograph for a fictional Brazilian gym management desktop application called Pulso. Create a premium natural photographic scene of a fictional adult Brazilian woman aged about 28, athletic and realistically proportioned, wearing charcoal gym clothing, doing a standing cable exercise in a beautiful contemporary gym. Horizontal 16:9 composition. Keep the person and equipment on the right third; left half shows dark atmospheric empty gym architecture and enough quiet negative space for white app text. Dark graphite walls, black rubber floor, warm sunlight through tall windows, subtle burnt orange detail on one piece of equipment. Realistic hands, realistic cable attachment and anatomically coherent exercise posture. Candid sports editorial, sophisticated natural texture, not a fashion ad. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no UI. Entire scene and person are fictional.
